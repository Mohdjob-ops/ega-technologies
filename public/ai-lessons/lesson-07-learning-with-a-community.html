Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

  • Explain why learning with other people can be helpful.
  • Identify useful activities inside a learning community.
  • Ask technical questions clearly and respectfully.
  • Support other learners without completing all their work.
  • Share progress, ideas, and achievements responsibly.
  • Follow good online-community behavior.
Main principle: You can study independently, but you do not have to solve every problem alone. A respectful learning community can provide encouragement, explanations, feedback, and new ideas.

English Technical Section

Learning software development can sometimes feel difficult. A learner may encounter errors, unfamiliar terminology, confusing instructions, or a problem that takes a long time to understand.

A learning community gives students a place to communicate with people who have similar goals. Members can ask questions, discuss lessons, exchange ideas, celebrate achievements, and encourage one another.

1

Ask Questions

A community can help when you are stuck, but your question should contain enough information for others to understand the problem.

A useful technical question may include:

  • What you are trying to do.
  • What you expected to happen.
  • What actually happened.
  • The exact error message.
  • The relevant code or screenshot.
  • What you have already tried.
2

Learn from Other Questions

You can learn even when you are not the person asking the question. Reading another learner's problem and solution may teach you how to avoid the same mistake.

Community discussions can also introduce different ways to solve the same programming problem.

3

Share Your Progress

Sharing a completed lesson, small project, certificate, or difficult problem you solved can strengthen your motivation.

Progress does not need to be perfect or dramatic. Completing one lesson or fixing one error is still meaningful progress.

4

Help Other Learners

Explaining a concept to another person can improve your own understanding.

Good help guides the learner toward the answer. It does not simply complete every assignment for them.

5

Receive Feedback

Other learners may notice unclear code, design problems, missing instructions, or mistakes that you did not see.

Feedback should be specific, respectful, and focused on improving the work rather than criticizing the person.

6

Stay Motivated

Seeing others learn and make progress can remind you that difficulty is a normal part of development.

A supportive community can encourage you to continue when a lesson or project feels challenging.

How to Ask a Good Community Question

Avoid questions that contain no useful detail. 

My code does not work. Help me.

A clearer question would be: 

I am trying to open the registration page from the Home screen.

Expected result:
Tapping the Register button should open /register.

Actual result:
Nothing happens when I tap the button.

File:
src/app/index.tsx

What I tried:
I restarted Expo and refreshed the browser.

Please help me identify the navigation problem.

The second question makes it easier for another learner or instructor to understand the problem.

How to Give Helpful Feedback

Weak feedback: 

Your code is bad.

Better feedback: 

The form works, but the labels are difficult to read on a small screen.

Consider increasing the label size and adding more space
between the fields.
Good feedback explains the issue and suggests a possible improvement without disrespecting the learner.

Qaybta Farsamada ee Af-Soomaaliga

Barashada software development-ka mararka qaarkood way adkaan kartaa. Ardaygu wuxuu la kulmi karaa errors, erayo cusub, amarro aan si fudud loo fahmin, ama dhibaato waqti dheer qaadata.

Bulshada waxbarashadu waxay isku xirtaa dad leh hadafyo isku dhow. Ardaydu way isweydiin karaan su'aalo, way wadaagi karaan fikrado, way isku caawin karaan, waxayna dhiirrigelin karaan horumarka midba midka kale.

1. Su'aalo Weydii

Marka aad dhibaato la kulanto, waxaad bulshada ka codsan kartaa caawimo. Laakiin su'aashaadu waa inay leedahay macluumaad ku filan.

Su'aal farsamo oo wanaagsan waxay sheegaysaa:

  • Waxa aad rabtay inaad samayso.
  • Waxa aad filaysay inuu dhaco.
  • Waxa dhab ahaan dhacay.
  • Error-ka saxda ah.
  • Code-ka ama screenshot-ka muhiimka ah.
  • Waxa aad hore u tijaabisay.

2. Su'aalaha Dadka Kale Wax Ka Baro

Xitaa haddii adigu aadan su'aasha weydiin, waxaad wax badan ka baran kartaa jawaabaha dadka kale la siiyo.

Dhibaatada qof kale maanta haysata waxay noqon kartaa dhibaato aad adigu berri la kulanto.

3. Horumarkaaga La Wadaag

Waxaad la wadaagi kartaa bulshada cashar aad dhammaysay, mashruuc yar, certificate, ama error aad xallisay.

Horumarku ma aha inuu mar walba noqdo mashruuc weyn. Hal cashar oo aad dhammaysay ama hal qalad oo aad saxday waa horumar muhiim ah.

4. Ardayda Kale Caawi

Marka aad fikrad qof kale u sharaxdo, fahamkaaga ayaa sii xoogaysan kara.

Caawinta wanaagsan waxay qofka tusaysaa jidka uu xalka ku gaari karo. Ma aha in shaqadiisa oo dhan loo qabto.

5. Feedback Qaado oo Bixi

Dadka kale waxay arki karaan khalad, design aan caddayn, ama meel la hagaajin karo oo aadan adigu arkin.

Feedback-gu waa inuu noqdaa mid gaar ah, xushmad leh, oo shaqada hagaajinaya.

6. Dhiirrigelinta Ilaali

Marka aad aragto arday kale oo la kulmay dhibaato laakiin ugu dambayn xalliyay, waxaad fahmaysaa in dhibku qayb ka yahay waxbarashada.

Fikradda muhiimka ah: Waxaad keligaaga wax ku baran kartaa, laakiin khasab ma aha inaad dhibaato kasta kaligaa xalliso.

Join the EGA Learning Community

Welcome to Elmi Guray Academy

EGA students are encouraged to participate in the official EGA learning community when access is provided.

The community can be used for:

  • Course announcements.
  • Lesson discussions.
  • Technical questions.
  • Student progress updates.
  • Project sharing.
  • Encouragement and respectful collaboration.

Joining and participating should remain optional. A student can continue learning even when they do not participate in every discussion.

Read Community Rules

EGA Community Rules

Be Respectful

Speak politely and respect learners from different backgrounds and skill levels.

Stay on Topic

Keep course channels focused on learning, programming, projects, and important EGA announcements.

Protect Private Information

Do not publicly share passwords, private keys, payment details, personal documents, or confidential student information.

Do Not Copy Assignments

Help learners understand the work, but do not submit another person's project as your own.

No Spam

Do not repeatedly send advertisements, unrelated links, or the same message many times.

Use Clear Questions

Include the error, relevant file, expected result, and what you already tried.

Never share passwords, API secret keys, banking information, identity documents, or private student records in a public learning group.

GAL — English Analogy

A Group of Travelers

Imagine several people traveling toward the same city. Each traveler carries their own bag and walks with their own feet, but they can still help one another.

One traveler may know the correct road. Another may have water. Another may notice danger. Another may encourage the group when the journey becomes difficult.

The community does not carry every student to the destination. Each student must continue walking and practicing.

A learning community supports the journey, but the learner must still complete the learning work.

Practical Activity

Activity 1: Improve the Question

Weak community question: 

My page is broken. What should I do?

Rewrite the question by adding:

  • The page or file.
  • The expected result.
  • The actual result.
  • The error message.
  • What has already been tried.
Show one possible answer 
I am working in src/app/register.tsx.

Expected result:
The registration form should save the student.

Actual result:
The page shows "Registration failed."

Error:
duplicate key value violates unique constraint.

What I tried:
I refreshed the page and entered a different phone number.

Which field may already exist in the database?

Activity 2: Choose Respectful Feedback

Which response is more helpful?

  1. Your project looks terrible.
  2. The project works, but the text is difficult to read because the background and text colors are too similar.
Show answer

Response 2 is more helpful.

It identifies a specific problem without insulting the learner.

Activity 3: Protect Private Information

Which information should never be posted publicly?

  • A general HTML example.
  • A secret API key.
  • A lesson title.
  • A public project screenshot without private data.
Show answer

A secret API key must never be posted publicly.

Activity 4: Write a Progress Message

Write a short community message explaining:

  • Which lesson you completed.
  • One new idea you learned.
  • One challenge you faced.
  • Your next learning goal.
Show example 
I completed Lesson 7 today.

I learned how to ask clearer technical questions.
My challenge was explaining the difference between the expected
result and the actual result.

My next goal is to complete the next lesson and practice
sharing one useful code example.

Multiple-Choice Quiz

1. What is one benefit of a learning community?

2. What should a good technical question include?

3. Which is an example of respectful feedback?

4. What should never be shared in a public group?

5. How should you help another learner?

6. Why can sharing progress be useful?

7. Is joining every community discussion required?

8. Who remains responsible for completing the learning work?

Open Answer Key
  1. B — Learners can exchange ideas and support.
  2. B — Include the expected result, actual result, and relevant details.
  3. B — It gives specific and respectful feedback.
  4. C — Never share a secret API key.
  5. A — Guide the learner toward understanding.
  6. A — Sharing progress can improve motivation.
  7. B — Participation may be optional.
  8. A — The learner remains responsible.

Remember

  • Learning with other people can provide encouragement, explanations, feedback, and new ideas.
  • A good technical question explains the goal, expected result, actual result, error, and previous attempts.
  • Respect learners at every skill level.
  • Helpful feedback identifies a specific problem and suggests an improvement.
  • Never share passwords, secret keys, payment data, or private student information.
  • Help others understand instead of completing all their work.
  • Sharing small achievements can improve motivation.
  • The community supports learning, but each learner must continue practicing.
