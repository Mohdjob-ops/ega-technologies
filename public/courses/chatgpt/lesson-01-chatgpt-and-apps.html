Introduction

ChatGPT Became More Powerful

ChatGPT can answer questions, explain ideas, help you write, and think through problems. Connected apps extend those abilities beyond the chat.

With an appropriate plugin or app connected, ChatGPT may search a supported service, create content, organize information, or prepare an action using the permissions you have granted.

Important: ChatGPT can use only apps that are available, connected, and authorized. The final capabilities depend on each app.

Technical Lesson

How ChatGPT Works With Apps

1

What Is a Plugin?

A plugin is an extension that gives ChatGPT additional capabilities. Depending on the plugin, it may include connected apps, reusable skills, templates, or specialized tools.

Plugins can support tasks such as productivity, creativity, communication, research, data analysis, travel planning, and project management.

2

ChatGPT Alone and ChatGPT With an App

Without a connected app, ChatGPT can explain how to complete a task and may provide useful public information. You usually perform the external action yourself.

With a suitable app connected, ChatGPT can use that app's available functions. For example, it may search a booking service, create a design, prepare a calendar event, or organize a task list.

Example request Find suitable hotel options in Sydney for September 18–21 with a budget of $120 per night.
3

Apps for Search and Creation

Different apps provide different capabilities. A booking app may search accommodations. A design app such as Canva may create a visual. A music app may recommend or prepare a playlist.

  • Travel: search and compare available options.
  • Calendar: prepare events using confirmed details.
  • Design: create visual drafts from a description.
  • Productivity: organize tasks and project information.
  • Music: recommend playlists for a mood or activity.
4

Permissions and User Control

Connecting an app does not give ChatGPT unlimited access. Each app has a defined scope, and private account information requires the permissions you explicitly grant.

  • Connect only apps you recognize and intend to use.
  • Read the requested permissions before approving them.
  • Check important details before confirming an external action.
  • Do not share passwords, security codes, or private keys in chat.
  • Disconnect an app when you no longer want it linked.

Rules

Rules for Connected Apps

  1. Use an app only when it is relevant to the task.
  2. Connect apps from a trusted and recognized source.
  3. Read requested permissions before approving access.
  4. Provide clear instructions and necessary limits.
  5. Verify names, dates, prices, and destinations carefully.
  6. Never provide passwords, private keys, or security codes.
  7. Review important actions before final confirmation.
Common Mistakes

Mistakes to Avoid

Expecting an unconnected app to work

ChatGPT cannot use an app that has not been installed or connected.

Choosing the wrong plugin

An app can perform only the actions supported within its scope.

Writing an incomplete request

Missing dates, budgets, locations, or style details can reduce accuracy.

Approving without reviewing

Always verify important information before confirming an external action.

Sharing sensitive information

Passwords and security codes should never be entered into a normal chat.

Remember

Important Points

  • Plugins add capabilities: they connect ChatGPT with supported tools.
  • Only connected apps work: unavailable apps cannot be used.
  • Every app has limits: capabilities depend on the app's scope.
  • You remain in control: review permissions and important actions.
  • Availability changes: new apps and features may be added over time.
